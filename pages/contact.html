Common Questions

What's your typical bot timeline?

Simple moderation bots: 1-2 weeks. Complex multi-feature bots: 3-4 weeks. I always provide a clear timeline upfront and ship incrementally so you see progress fast.

Do you only work with Discord and Telegram?

Discord and Telegram are my primary platforms, but I can adapt to other platforms if the API supports it. Let's talk about what you need.

What's your pricing?

Project-based for bot development, monthly retainer for ongoing community operations. Every engagement starts with a free discovery call to scope the work properly.
