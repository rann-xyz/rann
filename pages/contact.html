What's your typical project timeline?

Depends on scope. Simple bots: 1-2 weeks. Complex systems with dashboards: 4-8 weeks. Community strategy consulting: ongoing retainer. I always provide detailed timelines upfront.

Absolutely. While Web3 is my primary domain, the principles of community operations and AI automation apply everywhere. If you have a community problem, I can help solve it.

What's your pricing structure?

Project-based for development work, monthly retainer for ongoing community operations. Every engagement starts with a free discovery call to scope the work properly.
