Bots I've Designed

Every bot here solves a real problem I experienced while operating communities at Society.

🛡️
Discord Moderation Node.js

Sentry Moderator

Auto-moderation bot for Society Discord. Detects spam, filters toxic keywords, auto-warns repeat offenders, and escalates serious issues to human mods. Reduced manual moderation workload by 80%.

👋
Discord Onboarding Discord.js

Welcome Flow Bot

Automated onboarding for new Society members. Sends personalized welcome DMs, guides through verification, assigns roles based on interests, and introduces relevant channels. Zero manual onboarding needed.

📢
Telegram Broadcast Python

Society Broadcast

Cross-platform announcement bot connecting Discord and Telegram. Syncs important updates, handles scheduled posts, manages pinned messages, and tracks engagement metrics across both platforms.

⚙️
Discord Utility Node.js

Society Utility Suite

All-in-one utility bot — custom slash commands, role management, event creation with RSVP tracking, poll system, and member activity leaderboard. The Swiss Army knife of community tools.

🎫
Discord Support Node.js

Support Ticket Bot

Ticket management system for community support. Members open tickets via reactions, bots auto-categorize by issue type, and route to the right moderator. Average response time dropped from 4 hours to 30 minutes.

📊
Discord Analytics Python

Community Pulse

Analytics bot tracking community health metrics — active members, message velocity, engagement trends, and bot performance stats. Generates weekly reports so we know what's working and what needs fixing.
