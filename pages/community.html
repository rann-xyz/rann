// Approach

My Moderation Philosophy

🛡️

Proactive, Not Reactive

I build bots that prevent problems before they happen. Automated spam detection, smart verification, and predictive alerts keep Society safe 24/7.

📊

Data-Driven Decisions

Every moderation action is backed by data. I track engagement patterns, member lifecycle, and bot performance to continuously optimize.

🤝

Community-First

Rules exist to serve the community, not control it. I design systems that empower members while maintaining safety and order.
