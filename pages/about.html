// My Story

The Journey

I started as a community member — answering questions, helping newcomers, moderating chats. But I got tired of doing the same things over and over. Welcome messages, role assignments, spam cleanup. It was repetitive and unscalable.

So I learned to build bots. Not to become a full-stack developer, but to automate the boring stuff so I could focus on what actually matters — the people.

Today, I design bots and operate communities at Society. Every bot I build starts from a real pain point I experienced firsthand.

// Core Beliefs

Operating Principles

01

Bots Should Serve People

Every bot I design exists to make the community experience better — not to replace human connection, but to remove the friction that gets in the way.

02

Ship Fast, Fix Faster

A working bot in production beats a perfect spec on paper. I deploy quickly, gather feedback from real users, and iterate.

03

Automation + Human Touch

Bots handle the repetitive. Humans handle the meaningful. The best communities use tools to amplify connection, not replace it.

04

Design From Pain Points

I don't build features for the sake of features. Every bot starts with a real problem I or the community experienced.

05

Keep It Simple

Complex bots break. Simple bots scale. I prioritize reliability and maintainability over flashy features that nobody asked for.

06

Community-First

The community's needs come first. If a bot doesn't make members' lives easier, it doesn't ship.
