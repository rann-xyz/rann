// My Story

The Journey

I started where most builders do — frustrated with repetitive tasks. Moderating communities manually, answering the same questions, managing spreadsheets for whitelist allocations. It was exhausting and unscalable.

So I learned to code. Not to become a software engineer, but to automate the parts that didn't need human judgment. The first bot I built saved me 20 hours a week. That was the moment I realized: tools and community aren't separate — they're the same system.

Today, I operate at the intersection of AI tooling, Web3 infrastructure, and community governance. I've built automation systems serving 50,000+ community members and moderated communities from zero to thriving ecosystems.

// Core Beliefs

Operating Principles

01

Execution Over Theory

I don't whiteboard for months. I ship fast, gather feedback, and iterate. A working bot in production beats a perfect spec document every time.

02

Metrics That Matter

Community growth, engagement rates, automation efficiency. If it can't be measured, it can't be improved. Data drives every decision I make.

03

Decentralization First

Web3-native mindset. Community ownership, transparent governance, and tools that empower rather than control.

04

Automation + Human Touch

AI handles the repetitive. Humans handle the meaningful. The best communities use tools to amplify human connection, not replace it.

05

Ship Fast, Iterate Faster

Perfect is the enemy of shipped. I deploy MVPs in days, not months. Real-world usage teaches more than any planning session.

06

Community-First Design

Every tool I build starts with the community's actual needs, not what I think they need. User feedback is the first feature request.
